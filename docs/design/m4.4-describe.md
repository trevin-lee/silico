# M4.4: describe, each element's geometry and measures

`describe` is the kernel request behind M4.4's `elements` and `query` commands, and behind M4.5's measures (docs/ports/measure.md: one computation per number). This note covers the worker's request and the core's naming and ordering of what it returns. The commands' request and result shapes, and the query filters, are M4.1's (docs/design/m4.1-command-api.md, *Element entries*). This step changes no contract: the protocol's messages aren't one, and nothing here reaches a command yet.

## The request

`Op::Describe { shape, kind, first, count }` replies `Reply::Described { total, description }`: for the shape's elements of `kind` (faces, edges, or vertices) numbered from `first` (in OCCT's `TopExp::MapShapes` order, the numbering `Topology`, histories, and element maps use, from 1), `count` of them or as many as there are, what its geometry is and its measures, and how many of that kind the shape has. A request asks for at most `DESCRIBE_BATCH` (1,000), so no request nears the kernel's timeout (M4.1's `elements`); the core asks for batch after batch and joins them (`silico_protocol::describe_in_batches`, through a worker in `KernelWorker::describe` and in the kernel's own process in `silico_kernel::describe_whole`), refusing a kind's total that changes between batches. A batch from 0 or of solids is refused before it's sent.

**Faces.** Each face has:
- its surface type, OCCT's `GeomAbs_SurfaceType` through `BRepAdaptor_Surface`: plane, cylinder, cone, sphere, torus, Bézier, B-spline, revolution, extrusion, offset, or other;
- its area and centre of area, by `BRepGProp::SurfaceProperties` to the relative `MEASURE_PRECISION` (1e-9), M1's precise measure;
- for each kind of surface:
  - a plane: its unit normal, the surface's (Du × Dv: the plane's axis, or its opposite where the plane's frame is left-handed, as a chamfer's, a revolve's, and a mirrored shape's are), reversed if the face is reversed in the shape, so it points out of a solid, as FreeCAD's `Face.normalAt` has it. An internal or external face has no outside; its normal is its surface's;
  - a cylinder: its axis (a point on it and its unit direction, as the surface has it: of either sign, since only a plane's normal says which way a face faces; Silico's and FreeCAD's cylinders point theirs −z) and radius;
  - a cone: its axis, its radius at that point, and its half-angle in radians;
  - a sphere: its centre, as the axis's point, its axis direction, and its radius;
  - a torus: its axis, and its major and minor radii;
  - the rest: nothing more.

**Edges.** Each edge has:
- its curve type, OCCT's `GeomAbs_CurveType` through `BRepAdaptor_Curve`: line, circle, ellipse, hyperbola, parabola, Bézier, B-spline, offset, or other;
- its length, by `GCPnts_AbscissaPoint::Length` (as FreeCAD's `Edge.Length`), integrated to 1e-9 of a first estimate (OCCT's tolerance there is absolute);
- its start and end: its vertices' points in its curve's own order, as FreeCAD's `Edge.firstVertex()` and `lastVertex()` give them (not the orientation the face OCCT met it in first gives it, which an upstream edit reordering the faces can flip; and taken from the edge turned forward, so an internal or external edge, which a boolean can leave in a valid face, has its ends too);
- its middle, its curve at the middle of its parameter range;
- for each kind of curve:
  - a circle (whole or an arc): its axis (centre and unit normal) and radius;
  - an ellipse: its axis and its major and minor radii;
  - a Bézier or a B-spline: its degree and how many poles it has (so a two-pole one is known to be straight, as FreeCAD's `de14265ba7` has it);
  - the rest: nothing more.

A degenerated edge (a sphere's pole, a cone's apex: `BRep_Tool::Degenerated`) has no 3D curve. Its curve is `Degenerated`, its length 0, its start, end, and middle its vertex's point.

**Vertices.** Each vertex has its point.

A horn torus (its minor radius its major one, or more) is a surface of revolution to OCCT, not a torus, so it's described as one, with no radii: as OCCT and FreeCAD's `Part::Torus` have it.

**Checks.** The worker refuses a reply with a non-finite value as data, as `MeasureAsFreecad`'s check does (`worker.rs`). The core checks each batch before anything above it sees it (`Description::validate`, and that it holds what was asked for: its kind's elements, as many as there are from `first` up to `count`). One walk of a description's values serves both, so they name a value alike, by the kernel's number for its element (`the kernel's Face3's area`):
- every value is finite;
- every direction is a unit vector, within the mesh's `NORMAL_LENGTH_TOLERANCE`;
- every radius and length is positive (a degenerated edge's length is 0);
- every area is at least 0.

## Naming and order, in the core

`silico_core::elements::elements` takes a solid's `ElementMap` (M2.7) and the worker's description, and gives each element:
- its names (`ElementMap::names`), its own first: a reference by any of them finds it;
- the kernel's number for it;
- its kind, its type, and its measures.

The order is faces, then edges, then vertices, each by name, byte by byte: M4.1's order within one body (*elements*), so a model's list doesn't depend on OCCT's numbering, which can change when an upstream edit doesn't change the names.

Its counts are checked against the solid's element map when it's named (`silico_core::elements::elements`): a description of a solid with other counts is refused as a fault. That a description and a map are of one solid is the caller's to keep (the `elements` command takes both from one built step, M4.1).

When the `elements` command builds M4.1's entries (with M4.1), it turns the raw measures into its fields:
- `straight` is `EdgeCurve::is_straight`: a line, or a Bézier or B-spline of two poles (FreeCAD's `de14265ba7`);
- `midpoint` is the middle of a straight edge;
- `diameter` is twice a circle's, arc's, or cylinder's radius;
- `normal` is a plane's.

`default_x` is A4's, and comes with it.

## Decisions taken here (recommended, for Trevin)

1. **A degenerated edge** is listed (it's in the element map, by number) as an edge whose curve is `Degenerated` (written `other` in M4.1's entry), length 0, its ends its vertex. The alternatives are leaving it out, which would leave a name the map holds unlisted, or a type of its own, which M4.1's list of edge types doesn't have. Recommended: as described. FreeCAD lists it among `Shape.Edges`; its `Curve` raises for a sphere's pole and is a circle of radius about 0 for a cone's apex (8e-16 for FreeCAD's `Part::Cone` 4 to 0), and no entry's radius is 0, so `other` is right for both.
2. **A cone's measures** are its axis, its radius at the axis's point, and its half-angle. A cone's own position is where OCCT's `gp_Cone` puts it (its reference radius). Its apex is derived as the point where the radius would be 0, `apex = point − direction · radius / tan(half-angle)` (tested here). M4.1's entry gives both, `half_angle` (signed) and `apex`, `null` where the apex is past a length (a cone all but a cylinder): the `elements` command computes the apex from these (M4.1's follow-up, #126). Recommended: both, as OCCT and FreeCAD's `Part.Cone` (`Apex`, `Radius`, `SemiAngle`) do.
3. **A torus's radius** in M4.1's entry is `null`, since a torus has two. Its major and minor radii are in the raw measures, and M4.1's entry has `major_radius` and `minor_radius` for them (taken into #119).

## Known wrong results

OCCT's precise integration (`BRepGProp::SurfaceProperties` to a relative 1e-9, as M1's `summarize` measures areas and volumes too) is wrong on B-spline faces that are a curve swept straight, though it reports reaching its tolerance:
- **A cubic B-spline extruded** (a `SurfaceOfExtrusion`): of length 4.40405587355, extruded 2, its area is 2 × 4.40405587355 = 8.8081117471 by hand; OCCT gives 8.83202504977072, 0.27% over, saying it got to 2e-16. Closed by its chord and extruded, the solid's volume is exactly 3.8 (the region's area 1.9, by Gauss quadrature per knot span, exact for its polynomial integrand); `summarize` gives 3.796423264, 9.4e-4 low, from that face (its flat caps measure exactly 1.9).
- **A ruled B-spline surface** between two copies of a cubic B-spline 2 apart (a `BSplineSurface`): its area is 2 × 3.2682882396736437 = 6.536576479347287 (the length FreeCAD's `Edge.Length`, which an independent Gauss integration agrees with to 2.5e-13); OCCT gives 6.53657440325774, 3.2e-7 short, relatively.

OCCT's default integration (FreeCAD's `Face.Area`) isn't exact on them either: 8.808420588300923 (3.5e-5 over) and the same 6.53657440325774. On the fixture's Bézier and revolved faces both integrations agree with an independent Gauss integration of |Su × Sv|. Accepted, as OCCT's wrong results are (the fixture's test pins each, right or that answer), with areas staying OCCT's precise integration, as the maintainer chose it over FreeCAD's fixed default for accuracy (2026-10-09). Why the adaptive pass misses these is a follow-up (a span along the extrusion's boundary that isn't split at the curve's knots, most likely); Silico's own features make no such surface yet.

## Tests

Analytic, through the kernel worker:
- **A 10 × 20 × 30 box:**
  - six planar faces, areas 200, 300, and 600 twice each, each normal the axis it faces, each centre of area its face's middle;
  - twelve lines of lengths 10, 20, and 30, four each, their middles at their middles;
  - eight vertices at its corners.
- **A cylinder r 2.5, h 10:**
  - its side is a cylinder of radius 2.5 about the z axis, its direction −z, as FreeCAD 1.1.3's `Part::Cylinder` has it ((−0, −0, −1) on a reversed face; `Part.makeCylinder`'s is +z), area 50π;
  - its caps are planes of area 6.25π, normals −z and +z;
  - its rims are circles of radius 2.5, length 5π; its seam is a line of length 10.
- **A sphere r 3:** one spherical face of area 36π, centre (0, 0, 0); its degenerated pole edges are listed as degenerated, length 0.
- **The box's edges and vertices by number, each edge from its curve's start,** as FreeCAD 1.1.3 lists them; **batches:** the box's edges 5 to 7, its last edge alone, none past it, none from 0, and a 340-gon prism's 1,020 edges in two batches, each once, through a worker as in-process; **a moved sphere's** centre and poles; **the cut's hole rims** by name, circles about (20, 10) at z 0 and 10; **curves_and_surfaces.brep:** B-spline, Bézier, extrusion, and revolution faces and edges, each with FreeCAD's type, degree, poles, and length.
- **A cone r 4 to 0, h 6:** a conical face whose half-angle is atan(−4 / 6), −0.5880026 (narrowing up its axis, as FreeCAD 1.1.3's `Part::Cone` gives it), and whose apex is (0, 0, 6); r 0 to 4, +atan(4 / 6).
- **A torus R 10, r 2:** area 4π²Rr = 80π², radii 10 and 2.
- **A reversed face's normal** points out of the solid: a box's cut from a larger box, its inner faces' normals pointing into the hole.
- **`/break-it`'s** (crates/silico-kernel/tests/adversarial_describe.rs): chamfered boxes', revolves', and a mirrored box's planes face out, and their faces' Σ A·n is 0 and ⅓ Σ A (n · c) their volume (a proptest of boxes and prisms on any plane, combined); an internal edge a cut leaves is described with its ends; a slanted cut's ellipse; arcs of a turn; cones and tori at their limits; sizes at the tolerance and at a kilometre; garbage sent to the worker.
- **The core's names** are the element map's for a box (`k.box.Face1` to `Face6`) and for a cut. The order is faces, edges, vertices, each by name.
- **The same description, number for number, from two workers** (`elements.rs`); the determinism sweep doesn't yet run `describe` (with the `elements` command).
