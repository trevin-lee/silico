# M4.5 design: measures

M4.5 measures a model as the command API's section 7 gives it (docs/design/m4.1-command-api.md): `mass_properties`, `distance`, and `angle`. What each measure means was decided in docs/ports/measure.md (*Decided*, Fable, 2026-10-23, the maintainer's veto open), with the full record in `m4-measure-fable.md`. This note says how they're built, in three steps, each its own PR:

1. **`mass_properties`**, from each rebuild's summary, asking the kernel nothing (built).
2. **The protocol's `Op::Distance`**: OCCT's `BRepExtrema_DistShapeShape` on two elements in a worker, its every solution checked finite, "not done" a refusal as data.
3. **`distance` and `angle`** in the core: names resolved as M2.8 resolves a reference, the witness pair chosen by coordinates, `center_distance` and `normal_distance` from `describe`'s measures, and the angles by `libm`'s `atan2`.

## `mass_properties`

`command::measure::mass_properties` gives each solid's volume, area, and centre of mass from its rebuild's summary (M1's `measure`, OCCT's integration asked for a relative 1e-9, decision 4, with the known wrong results docs/ports/measure.md lists; volume and area as the rebuilt fingerprint holds them, to a millionth of a mm³ and mm², so a micrometre cube's volume is 0; the centre, which no fingerprint holds, to the nanometre): a feature's solid, built, consumed or not, or each body (the solids no later feature consumed, `Model::bodies`) in timeline order. A session runs it on the model a read names, current or published (`Sessions::mass_properties`, decision 33). Its wire types are `command::wire::measure`, its snapshot `tests/contract/commands/mass_properties.json`.
- **The fingerprint's numbers.** The volume and area are `Fingerprint::of`'s, the one rounding of a summary to 6 decimals, so a measure and the document's fingerprint never disagree; the centre is rounded to the nanometre (`Length::rounded`).
- **Refused as `elements` refuses a solid** (`command::elements::built`, shared): a feature the document doesn't have, a sketch, or one blocked, naming `feature` when the request named it; one that failed gives its failure. A solid whose summary the kernel refused gives that refusal, naming its feature. Every solid that can't be measured is said, in timeline order, the first 100 listed and all counted (`Problems`).
- **Why no kernel request:** each built feature's solid is summarized as it's rebuilt (M2.6, M4.4's `Engine::measure`), and a published model holds every summary, so a read of it costs nothing and never waits on a worker.
- **Tests:** a box, a cylinder, and a sphere, each body's three numbers by hand from its dimensions; M2.1's example (its block, consumed, its pin, and the cut), by hand; the design's example written byte for byte from the example rebuilt on workers; on the fake kernel, the bodies' order, the numbers equal to the fingerprints', every refusal, and the session's choice of model.

## The angle reads 30 for a 30° wedge

Trevin's choice (2026-10-25, relayed by Main): `angle` is built exactly as the decided formula gives it, `atan2(|n1 × n2|, −n1 · n2)` over two planar faces' outward normals, with no flip. A 30° wedge's two faces read 30, and the test that pins it cites this choice.
