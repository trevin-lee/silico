# M3 – Sketcher

**Status: detailed** from the study, docs/ports/sketcher.md. M3 runs in parallel with M2. M3.2 onward change the document's schema (a protected contract): each such step starts from a design the maintainer approves, as M2.1 did. M3.6 needs M2.7–M2.8's names for sketch edges.

**Goal:** constrained 2D sketches, solved by PlaneGCS in a worker, that turn into faces and drive pad, pocket, and revolve.

**Exit tests:** the ported FreeCAD sketcher and face-maker tests pass, interactive drag latency through the worker is measured, and the council approves with `/adversarial-review --milestone`.

## Study

**`docs/ports/sketcher.md`** (done), from FreeCAD's sketcher (`SketchObject*.cpp`, `Sketch.cpp`, `Constraint.cpp`, `SketchAnalysis.cpp`), PlaneGCS, `FaceMakerBullseye` and `FaceMakerCheese`, PartDesign's profile, pad, pocket, and revolve, their tests, and their history. Its main findings, which shape the steps:
- **PlaneGCS at FreeCAD 1.1.3**, the oracle's release, not main: main changed the solver's semantics (signed distances, how reference values are found, the conflict analysis) and has a status regression 1.1.3 doesn't.
- **Determinism needs more than the recipe:** the solver orders its unknowns by their addresses, and Eigen's matrix products block by the CPU's cache sizes on x86. The worker allocates every parameter from one array in a canonical order, and fixes Eigen's cache sizes.
- **FreeCAD's validation is weak** (out-of-range references hide every constraint; anything else surfaces as "malformed" at solve time), and several choices are made from the current geometry. Silico's format makes each constraint form and each choice explicit, and the gate refuses what doesn't fit.
- **FreeCAD tests almost none of the solve's outcomes** (degrees of freedom, conflicting, redundant): Silico writes those cases by hand count and checks them through the oracle.

## Steps

| Step | Builds | Key tests |
|---|---|---|
| M3.1 PlaneGCS worker | PlaneGCS from FreeCAD 1.1.3's source archive (pinned by SHA-256, shared between checkouts as OCCT's build is), Eigen 3.4.0 and Boost's headers from pixi, built with the deterministic recipe (`-ffp-contract=off`, `EIGEN_DONT_VECTORIZE`, the libm shim) and `NDEBUG`, and its determinism patches (the dependent-parameter thread made deferred). A `silico-sketch` crate: a `cxx` bridge that owns one parameter array in canonical order and fixes Eigen's cache sizes, and a `silico-sketch-worker` binary serving solve requests (geometry, constraints with tags from 1, the result: status, the solved parameters, degrees of freedom, and conflicting, redundant, and partially redundant tags). Requests in the solver's terms: points, lines, circles, arcs and the constraints a rectangle and its variants need; the document's sketch comes in M3.2–M3.3 | A constrained rectangle solves to its corners (analytic); a free line has 4 degrees of freedom; an exception in the solver becomes `Err`; non-finite input is refused and non-finite output never leaves the worker; the worker crash, hang, and garbage suite from M0.7, through the same client; the math-import allowlist check passes on the worker; a randomized sketch stress corpus, including one past Eigen's blocking threshold, is bit-identical across runs and between the Mac and the Linux runner, against a committed reference |
| M3.2 Sketch model | A design for the maintainer first (`docs/design/m3.2-sketch-format.md`): points, lines, circles, arcs, ellipses, B-splines, construction, and every constraint *form* explicit (point–point distance, point–line distance, line length, …) with its choices stored (tangency direction, side); stable IDs; coordinates as lengths, angles as angles. Then the types, reader, canonical writer, schema snapshot, and the validation gate's sketch rules | Schema round trip and canonical bytes; the gate refuses a constraint naming missing geometry, a reference of the wrong kind or position, a radius ≤ 0, a distance of 0, a non-finite or out-of-range value, a dimension on fixed geometry only, and a degenerate element, each naming the constraint and field (FreeCAD's `testValidateConstraintsCase`, and the cases it lacks) |
| M3.3 Solving and diagnostics | The core translates a checked sketch into a worker request (every form to its PlaneGCS rows, tags from positions, a diameter as half its value as a radius); FreeCAD's order: diagnose, then DogLeg, Levenberg–Marquardt, BFGS, and SQP; outcomes as data: over-constrained, conflicting (naming constraint IDs), redundant (solved and kept, the redundancy reported, as SolidWorks does), solver failure, invalid result (an arc that flipped, ellipse radii that crossed). A sketch case in the oracle harness (headless FreeCAD's `sketch.solve()`, `DoF`, and constraint lists) | Ported `TestSketcherSolver.py` cases (rectangle, box, slot, circle–line distance, reference read-back) and `TestCoincidentCommandGui.py`'s solver fixtures; hand-counted degrees of freedom, conflicting, redundant, and partially redundant cases, confirmed by the oracle and kept as protected oracle cases; `tangentBSplineAndArc` with its angle imposed |
| M3.4 Stored coordinates | Solved coordinates written to the file, used as the solver's start, re-solved on load when the constraints changed | A hand edit to a constraint triggers a re-solve, and `fmt` writes back the corrected coordinates; re-solving an unchanged sketch leaves the file's bytes unchanged on every checked platform |
| M3.5 Profile to face | Bullseye's behavior: closed wires (refusing open ones, T-junctions, and figure-8s, naming the elements), nesting by containment to any depth (holes and islands), orientation; crossing, overlapping, and touching wires refused (a divergence from FreeCAD, which doesn't check). The core decides from the solved geometry; the worker builds the faces | Ported `FaceMakerBullseye` tests (areas analytic); `/break-it` catalog cases for open, self-intersecting, crossing, and touching profiles, lone points, and construction geometry left out |
| M3.6 Sketch-driven features | Pad, pocket, and revolve (and groove) from sketches, as timeline features: depth > 0 with `reversed`, symmetric and two-sided as one prism, through-all pocket, revolve angle in (0°, 360°] about a sketch axis or construction line that mustn't cross the profile. Needs M2.7–M2.8 (sketch edges named from their IDs): coordinated with M2 before it starts | Ported `TestPad`, `TestPocket`, and `TestRevolve` cases and PartDesign's `Pad.cpp` and `Revolution.cpp`, as oracle cases with analytic volumes (pocket 75, two-sided pad 4 and 3000π, through-all 62.5, torus segments), refine set explicitly |
| M3.7 Drag latency | A drag session in the sketch worker (begin: diagnose once and pin the dragged element at lower priority; move: DogLeg from the start state; end: a full solve), and a benchmark of the round trip during a simulated drag | p50 and p99 latency recorded. If p99 exceeds the frame budget, decide between shared-memory IPC and an in-process solver, and record the decision in ARCHITECTURE.md |
